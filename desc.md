# StelluAsuna - Game Overview

StelluAsuna is a comprehensive modpack/game for Luanti (Minetest) that represents a unique fusion of two major overhauls: **Stellua** and **Asuna**. By bridging these ecosystems, the game offers a massive, varied, and immersive voxel sandbox experience with an unparalleled amount of content ranging from new dimensions and magic to intricate farming and bustling villages.

## Core Integration
At the heart of the game are the primary core mods:
- **Asuna Core**: Provides the foundational framework from the Asuna game/modpack.
- **Stellua Suite**: Includes `stl_core`, `stl_decor`, `stl_precursor`, `stl_vehicles`, and `stl_weather`.
- **stl_asuna_bridge**: A custom bridge mod specifically designed to seamlessly interlock the features, generation, and mechanics of Stellua and Asuna.

## Additional Mods and Expansions

Beyond the core fusion, StelluAsuna is heavily expanded with numerous other mods to enrich every aspect of gameplay:

### 🌌 Dimensions & Exploration
The universe is vast, allowing players to explore beyond the Overworld:
- **Astralcraft & Cloudcraft**: Take to the skies and beyond.
- **Nether & Caverealms**: Deep, treacherous underground dimensions.
- **Everness & Other Worlds**: Expansive, fantastical new realms to discover.
- **SGJourney (Stargate Journey) & Worldgate**: Advanced portal technologies for dimension and interstellar travel.

### 🌿 Biomes, Flora & Fauna
The world generation is incredibly lush and alive:
- **Biomes & NaturalBiomes**: A huge variety of new landscapes.
- **LivingCaves, LivingJungle, Ethereal**: Highly detailed, immersive specific biomes.
- **Animalia, Mobs, LivingSlimes**: A rich ecosystem of peaceful and hostile creatures.
- **Farming, X_Farming, Herbs, Plantlife Modpack**: Extensive agriculture and gathering systems.

### 🏰 Towns, NPCs & Structures
The world feels lived-in with dynamic inhabitants:
- **MG_Villages & Working_Villages**: Procedurally generated towns with functional NPCs.
- **AliveAI**: Advanced artificial intelligence for entities.
- **DungeonsPlus & Lootchests Modpack**: Rewarding exploration and combat encounters.

### ⚔️ Survival, Magic & Mechanics
Gameplay is significantly deepened:
- **3D Armor & Armor Monoid**: Comprehensive equipment systems.
- **Stamina & Soup**: Advanced hunger and survival mechanics.
- **Effervescence & Magic**: Mystical elements and spellcraft.
- **Horror & Skeletons**: Enhanced nighttime and underground threats.
- **Logistica & Researcher**: Tech-like automation and progression.

### 🎨 Quality of Life & Decor
- **Wielded Light, Show Wielded Item, Item Drop**: Visual enhancements for better immersion.
- **Colorful Beds, Carpets, ABDecor**: Expansive interior decoration options.
- **Music Modpack**: Enhances the atmosphere with custom soundtracks.

StelluAsuna is a true mega-pack, harmonizing the futuristic and fantasy elements of its parent mods into one cohesive, endlessly replayable adventure.

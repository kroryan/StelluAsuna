# Original StelluAsuna Stargate assets

The PNG textures and OGG sounds shipped in this directory were created for
the private StelluAsuna Luanti game on 2026-08-16. They are not copied from
Stargate Journey or from the Stargate audiovisual works.

The texture source is `original_texture_atlas.png`, generated from an original
prompt requesting non-franchise voxel pixel art. Individual 16x16 textures are
derived from that atlas. The OGG files are locally synthesized waveforms and
noise, with no sampled audio.

under CC0 1.0 Universal. You may copy, modify, redistribute, or replace them
without attribution.
